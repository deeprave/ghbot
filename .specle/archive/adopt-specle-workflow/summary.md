# Summary: Adopt Specle workflow

## Summary

ghbot now uses the supported Specle workflow, with project-specific guidance retained and its historical change record preserved in the Specle inventory.

## Outcome

The repository has a single durable workflow and lifecycle model. Historical completed work is archived, while future work uses the selected change record and project overlay.

## What changed

- Replaced the copied portable workflow with the supported Specle skills and policies.
- Migrated historical change artifacts into the Specle inventory and archived the completed records.
- Recorded ghbot's delivery, review, language, and automation conventions as project guidance.

## Key decisions and limits

Historical delivery evidence was retained where available and not reconstructed. The archived records preserve the completed work history without fabricating unavailable evidence.
